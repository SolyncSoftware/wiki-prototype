Hello, I am papertek. It's actually very early in the morning on a Thursday. Today, I announce that this version of the Camellia Wiki will cease operations on December 7th, 2024. This is because I presented the Camellia Server staff with a contract on transferring ownership to them, as they do not want me to own this website. They refused the terms in the contract, so now this site will shut down. I apologize for the inconvenience.

Thank you for the support throughout the one year and few months this website was up. This started off as a school project and turned into a really big thing, I didn't expect we'll get this far.

It's a shame, but the conditions of the contract were not met, and my interest in this community has dwindled because of constant tensions, even while minding my own business.

Thank you for understanding and have a nice day/night. Take care of yourself.

## Overview

![KCCD-003](/assets/images/camellia/albums/KCCD-003.jpg)
りぷれい！ is a doujin album by [かめりあ](/camellia) feat. [ななひら](#) released on 2015-08-16 (Comiket 88) by the circles [かめるかめりあ](#) & [Confetto](#).

It is the second collaboration album between _Camellia_ and _Nanahira_.

## Overview

![CTCD-003](/assets/images/camellia/albums/CTCD-003.jpg)
_mikUbiquity_ is a doujin album by [かめりあ](</camellia>) (Camellia) released on 2011-10-30 (M3-28) by the circle [かめるかめりあ](#).

It is the third [Vocaloid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vocaloid) album by _Camellia_. On 2019-12-31 the CD was re-released as part of the _Camellia Archives_ series of re-releases.

## Overview

![KCCD-007](/assets/images/camellia/albums/KCCD-007.png)
ごーいん！ is a doujin album by [かめりあ](</camellia>) feat. [ななひら](#) released on 2019-08-12 (Comiket 96) by the circles [かめるかめりあ](#) & [Confetto](#).

It is the fifth collaboration album between _Camellia_ and _Nanahira_.

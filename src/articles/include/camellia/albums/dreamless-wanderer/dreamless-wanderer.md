## Overview

![CTCD-010](/assets/images/camellia/albums/CTCD-010.png)
_dreamless wanderer_ is a doujin album by [かめりあ](/camellia) (Camellia) released on 2014-12-30 (Comiket 87) by the circle [かめるかめりあ](#).

It is the fourth intrumental album by _Camellia_. In the tracklist of the original CD release, the titles of tracks 2, 3 and 4 are incorrectly swapped.

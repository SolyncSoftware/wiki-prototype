## Overview

![KCCD-006](/assets/images/camellia/albums/KCCD-006.png)
ふぉーす！ is a doujin album by [かめりあ](/camellia) feat. [ななひら](#) released on 2017-12-29 (Comiket 93) by the circles [かめるかめりあ](#) & [Confetto](#).

It is the fourth collaboration album between _Camellia_ and _Nanahira_.

## Overview

![KCCD-001](/assets/images/camellia/albums/KCCD-001.png)
ばーさす！ is a doujin album by [Camellia](/camellia) feat. [Nanahira](#) released on 2014-08-17 (Comiket 86) by the circle [Confetto](#).

It is the first collaboration album between _Camellia_ and _Nanahira_.

## Overview

![CTCD-007](/assets/images/camellia/albums/CTCD-007.jpg)
_paroxysm_ is a doujin album by [かめりあ](/camellia) (Camellia) released on 2013-12-31 (Comiket 85) by the circle [かめるかめりあ](#).

It is the first intrumental album by _Camellia_. On 2019-12-31 the CD was re-released as part of the _Camellia Archives_ series of re-releases. In the tracklist of the original CD release, the titles of tracks 1 and 2 are incorrectly swapped.

## Overview

![CTCD-008](/assets/images/camellia/albums/CTCD-008.jpg)
_\[diffraction\]_ is a doujin album by [かめりあ](/camellia) (Camellia) released on 2014-04-27 (M3-33) by the circle [かめるかめりあ](#).

It is the second intrumental album by _Camellia_. On 2019-12-31 the CD was re-released as part of the _Camellia Archives_ series of re-releases.

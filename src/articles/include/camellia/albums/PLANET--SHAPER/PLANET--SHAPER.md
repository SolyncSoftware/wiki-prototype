## Overview

![CTCD-011](/assets/images/camellia/albums/CTCD-011.jpg)
_PLANET//SHAPER_ is a doujin album by [かめりあ](/camellia) (Camellia) released on 2015-08-16 (Comiket 88) by the circle [かめるかめりあ](#).

It is the fifth intrumental album by _Camellia_.

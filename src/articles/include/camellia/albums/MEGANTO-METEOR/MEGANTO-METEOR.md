## Overview

![QWCE-90001](/assets/images/camellia/albums/QWCE-90001.png)
_MEGANTO METEOR_ is a major album by [かめりあ](/camellia) (Camellia) released on 2016-07-06 on the label [EXITTUNES Dance Production](#).

It is the first major album by _Camellia_.
